# Visual Treatment Rubric — 100 points

Use this rubric to judge visual polish only. Do not score story/caption wording/narration quality when those elements are locked.

| Dimension | Weight | What good looks like |
|---|---:|---|
| Visual hierarchy & focal control | 20 | Viewer immediately knows where to look; emphasis follows meaning |
| Motion purpose & restraint | 15 | Effects explain, focus, or transition; no decorative noise |
| UI/demo clarity | 15 | Product behavior remains readable; cause/action/result is visually traceable |
| Transition consistency | 10 | Small coherent transition vocabulary; cuts/transitions fit semantic beats |
| Composition, crop & framing | 10 | Mobile-friendly framing; relevant regions are legible; safe zones respected |
| Temporal pacing & rhythm | 10 | Visual changes support attention without rushing reading or creating fatigue |
| Caption protection/readability | 10 | Effects never fight captions; contrast and safe zones remain strong |
| Depth, overlays & finish | 5 | Subtle depth/polish improves separation without looking gimmicky |
| Before/After communication | 5 | Transformation is visually obvious when applicable; no invented claims |
| **Total** | **100** | |

## Severity labels
- **P0** — effect obscures content/captions, fabricates behavior, breaks locked timing, or materially harms comprehension.
- **P1** — major focal/pacing inconsistency that makes the video feel amateur or confusing.
- **P2** — visible polish opportunity with meaningful quality impact.
- **P3** — optional refinement.

## Premium threshold
A score of 90+ requires not merely more motion but disciplined restraint: coherent motion grammar, clean focal control, readable UI, protected captions, and no obvious decorative effect that can be removed without loss.
